Quiet Shelf 0.17.0 improves library display and study-book filtering, and publishes the original project under Apache License 2.0.

- Alphabetical sorting orders series by their names and their books by title. Add independent Show series / Show standalones filters and a reversible Plain list view, with preferences retained locally, in backups and through Drive sync.
- Look up missing information when generating a list, from saved catalog edition links and exact ISBNs. Read Kitapyurdu product categories, including Dil Eğitimi, to identify study books. Never classify a book as a textbook from its title or language alone; label unconfirmed types and keep cached offline generation. Flag previously misclassified saved-list entries without replacing the checklist automatically.
- Add reversible exclusions for individual titles or entire series, with optional end dates or no expiry. Review and undo rules under Read next → Excluded titles & series. Series rules cover later volumes; expiry changes future eligibility without rewriting the current checklist. Rules, dates and view options persist in backups and Drive, separately by workspace.
- Add the standard Apache-2.0 LICENSE and a NOTICE crediting Alex (Ayca Kaygusuz) as Quiet Shelf’s original creator. The grant covers original project material, including earlier published revisions; third-party material retains its respective rights and terms.
- Account → About Quiet Shelf shows the creator credit, full Apache license, attribution, third-party notices and Manrope OFL text offline across Android, Windows and browser.
- Browser packages, Android assets and Windows installations retain the applicable license/notice files. Windows also keeps its Electron/Chromium runtime notices. Existing published binaries remain unchanged; new packages use version 0.17.0 and preserve user data.
